# Dope card BC header

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Branch:** task/142-bc-header
- **Issue:** [#142](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/142)
- **Status:** Not in production yet

## Summary

Both dope cards printed the stored `bc` and `bcType` as they were, in the BC field of the header. Neither field has a length limit, so a very long stored text was measured and cut on every card. The header could also disagree with the card: the trajectory reads the BC with `readDecimal` and uses G1 for any table other than `G7`.

The BC field now prints what the card computed with: `bcHeaderText(profile)` in [`dopeCardLayout.js`](../src/lib/utils/dopeCardLayout.js) gives `readDecimal(bc)` and `G7` or `G1`. Both `dopeCardImperial.js` and `dopeCardMetric.js` use it. A card exists only when that BC is usable (`isUsableBc`), so the number is always finite and at least 0.01.

| Stored | Printed before | Printed now |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| `0.243`, `G7` | `0.243 G7` | `0.243 G7` |
| `0,243`, `G7` | `0,243 G7` | `0.243 G7` |
| `0.4620`, `G1` | `0.4620 G1` | `0.462 G1` |
| `0.5`, `G7x` | `0.5 G7x` | `0.5 G1` |
| 100 000 characters of `bc`, 1 MB of `bcType` | cut with an ellipsis | the parsed number and `G1` |

**Release note:** the BC on the dope card is printed as the card used it (decimal point, no trailing zeros).

No calculated result changed. No baseline changed.
