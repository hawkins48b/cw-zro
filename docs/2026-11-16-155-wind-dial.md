# Wind dial: thicker arrow, larger dial, 15° touch points

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Branch:** task/155-wind-dial
- **Issue:** [#155](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/155)
- **Status:** Delivered, not in production yet. Not checked in a browser or on a device

## Summary

The owner asked for a thicker, shorter wind arrow that does not cover the degree labels, a larger dial on mobile too, and points every 15° for quick setting by touch.

The dial is used by `ScopeWindCard`, so the change shows on the Scope View and the Ballistic page.

## What changed

| Part | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Size | 112 px, beside the fields from 320 px of card width | Up to 240 px (full card width when narrower), above the fields; beside them from 448 px of card width, at 192 px |
| Arrow | 1.5 wide, radius 37 head to 22 tail, Wind icon at radius 32 (over the 180° label at some angles) | 3.5 wide shaft, filled head; head tip at radius 21, Wind icon from radius 11.5 to 22.5 (viewBox 100) |
| Labels | 0° / 90° / 180° / 270° centred at radius 26 | Centred at radius 31; the inner edge of "270°" is at about 23, so the arrow never reaches them |
| Rim | 12 ticks | 24 points every 15°, larger on the clock hours; 0° stays the orange downrange marker |
| Current direction | Arrow only | Its point is filled blue when the direction is a multiple of 15° (the 0° marker gets a blue ring) |
| Pointer | Any tap or drag sets whole degrees | From 70 % of the radius outwards it snaps to the nearest 15°; inside, whole degrees as before |
| Keys | Arrows ± 5° | Arrows ± 5°, Page Up / Page Down ± 15°, Home 0° (`aria-keyshortcuts`) |

The snap is `dialDirection(dx, dy, radius)` in [`windDirection.js`](../src/lib/utils/windDirection.js), with `DIAL_SNAP_DEGREES = 15` and `DIAL_SNAP_RIM = 0.7`. The rim zone starts well inside the points (radius 0.86), so a finger does not have to hit a point.

Developer's choices, not confirmed by the owner: the 70 % rim, the 448 px breakpoint, the sizes, and the extra keys.

## Results

No calculated result changed. The dial still writes whole degrees in [0, 360) as text into the direction box, which `shotWinds` reads as before (#106 convention: where the wind comes from). No baseline changed, no new message.

## Security review

PASS, no findings in the change. Pre-existing (Low, not fixed): with `Infinity` or `1e999` in the direction box, a key step or the − / + buttons send the text `NaN`, and a typed fraction stays a fraction after a step. The pointer never does this.
