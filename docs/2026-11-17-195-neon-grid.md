# 3D tutorial: neon-grid look, backstop, visible target, even grass

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Branch:** task/195-neon-grid
- **Issue:** [#195](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/195), follow-up to [#182](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/182) in epic [#180](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/180) (3D ballistic tutorial)
- **Status:** Delivered with known issues. The new look has never been seen: the dev container has no WebGL, and the cw-lab rule says no browser tests by agents. The owner has to check it in a browser (list below). Nothing was run in Electron, and the cost of bloom on a real graphics card is unknown

## Summary

After #182 the owner reported four problems with the scene: the target could not be seen from the shooter view, there was no backstop behind it, the grass was uneven, and the style looked like a game. The owner wanted a cyberpunk, digital look instead.

**Delivered.**

- **Neon-grid look.** Near-black sky with a glow at the horizon, black ground with cyan grid lines every 1 m and 10 m that follow the slope, models as dark solids with neon edge lines, a magenta tracer for the bullet, and bloom (glow) from Medium quality up. No lights and no shadows.
- **Wind cues kept.** Grass, leaves and flag cloth keep their shapes and their sway, drawn as glowing wireframes, so the wind tutorial (#189) can still use them.
- **Four weathers** as digital variants: clear, haze, neon rain, glowing snow. Visual only; the stored values (`sunny`, `cloudy`, `rain`, `snow`) are unchanged.
- **Backstop** behind the target, sized from the distance.
- **Target easy to find** from the shooter view: a HUD bracket with the distance ("Target 300 m"), a glowing outline with the zones, and a narrower field of view (32° instead of 50°).
- **Even grass** over the whole range.
- One new message, `tutorial_hud_target`, in English, French and German.

The guides describe the parts in detail: [3D tutorial](architecture.md#3d-tutorial) in the architecture guide (neon-grid table, finding the target, grass). This document does not repeat them.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-17)

| Topic | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| Look | Neon grid |
| Grass and trees | Keep their shapes and sway, as glowing wireframes (for #189) |
| Weathers | The four choices stay, as digital variants: clear, haze, neon rain, glowing snow. Stored values unchanged |
| Target visibility | "Go for the better experience": HUD bracket with distance, glowing outline and zones, and a narrower shooter field of view, all three |

## Defaults chosen during the task, not yet confirmed by the owner

| Topic | Default |
|-------|---------|
| Grass count | Fixed per quality (1,500 Low, 6,000 Medium, 16,000 High), spread over the whole range. Density therefore falls with the distance: about one tuft per 9 m² on Low and 0.9 m² on High at 300 m, 88 m² and 8 m² at 3000 m |
| Colours | Cyan edges on rifle, sandbag, trees and flag poles; magenta on the optics, tracer, backstop and flag cloth; green grass and leaves; pale yellow for highlighted parts. Target outline cyan, zones A magenta, C cyan, D dim cyan |
| Backstop | 3 m behind the target, 3% of the distance wide (at least 6 m), 2% of it high above the target's ground (at least 3.5 m), 1 m into the ground |
| Shooter view | 32° vertical field of view, looking straight at the target |
| HUD bracket | At least 28 px, 6 px around the target; hidden when it would be larger than 160 px, through the optic, off screen or behind the camera |
| Bloom | Off on Low, half the drawing size on Medium, full size on High. Strength 1.1, radius 0.45, threshold 0.05 |
| Lights | None: every material is unlit, so there are no shadows (the quality presets lose `shadows` and `shadowMapSize`) |

## How to use

Nothing changes in the steps of #182. Open **Calculators**, then **3D ballistic tutorial** (`/calculators/ballistic-tutorial`, web and desktop only), press **Open the 3D scene**, and the scene shows in the new look. In the Shooter camera the bracket around the target shows its distance in the range unit. Weather and quality are picked in the Scene card as before.

## Technical changes

- **Scene helpers:** [`src/lib/utils/tutorialScene.js`](../src/lib/utils/tutorialScene.js). New: `NEON` palette, `WEATHER_LOOKS` rewritten (sky, horizon glow, fog, grid colour and strength, precipitation colour), `QUALITY_PRESETS` with `bloom` and `bloomScale` instead of shadows, `grassBand`, `grassGrid` and a jittered-grid `grassPlacements` (one tuft per cell, 5% from the cell's sides, sandbag and target stand kept clear), `backstopBox` and `BACKSTOP_GAP`, `targetFaceCorners`, `hudBracket` with `HUD_MIN_SIZE` and `HUD_MAX_SIZE`, `SHOOTER_FOV_DEG`; the shooter pose now looks at the aim point, orbit centre 25 m out.
- **Scene:** [`src/lib/tutorial/scene.js`](../src/lib/tutorial/scene.js). Sky dome and ground grid shaders, `neonize` (edge lines as children named `edges`, faces meeting at more than 25°, so every model node name of #181 stays), wireframe grass, leaves and cloth, target outline and zones, backstop wall, tracer, highlight on edge lines (pulsing pale yellow), bloom with `EffectComposer`, `RenderPass`, `UnrealBloomPass` and `OutputPass` from the three.js addons (made the first time bloom is on, freed in `dispose()`), and the HUD bracket placed each frame. Still the only module that imports three.js.
- **Component:** [`TutorialScene.svelte`](../src/lib/components/tutorial/TutorialScene.svelte) gets a bindable `hud` element (the bracket, `aria-hidden`, no pointer events) and `hudLabel`.
- **Page:** [`ballistic-tutorial/+page.svelte`](../src/routes/calculators/ballistic-tutorial/+page.svelte) passes the bracket to `createTutorialScene` (`hud` option) and builds the label with `m.tutorial_hud_target`.
- **Messages:** `tutorial_hud_target` ("Target {distance}", "Cible {distance}", "Ziel {distance}"); 991 keys per language.
- **Tests:** [`src/test/tutorialScene.test.js`](../src/test/tutorialScene.test.js) adds checks for the quality presets (bloom off on Low), the four weather looks, even grass (covers the whole range, clear areas, no count), the shooter view (narrower, optic view still ±6 mrad), the backstop (frames the target from 25 to 3000 m and at ±500 m elevation, grows with the distance), the HUD bracket and the target corners, and the palette.
- **Guides updated:** [architecture](architecture.md) (neon-grid look, finding the target, grass, bundle sizes, CSP note), [development](development.md) (models are drawn in the neon look; their materials are not used), [testing](testing.md) (what of the scene is not tested), [translations](translations.md) (key count). Checked against the code by the documentor.

No calculated result, baseline or Content-Security-Policy changed. `three` stays at 0.187.0.

## Security review

**PASS** in 1 round. The bloom addons use shaders and render targets only (no worker, no `blob:` URL, no network), and the bracket is placed with `element.style`, which the CSP does not restrict. The label is rendered as text by Svelte.

## Verification

- `npm run test:run`: 5210 passed, 1 todo, in 76 files (documentor's run).
- `npm run build` and `npm run build:mobile`: developer's run.

Not verified:

- **The whole look.** `scene.js` has never run: jsdom and the dev container have no WebGL, and agents do not test in a browser. Shaders, edge lines, bloom, wireframes, weathers and the HUD placement are not covered by tests.
- Electron.
- Performance with bloom on Medium and High.

The owner should check, in a desktop browser:

1. Shooter view at 25, 1000 and 3000 m, and with the target at +400 m and -400 m: the target is easy to find, the bracket shows the right distance, the backstop stands behind the target and frames it.
2. The look: dark sky with a horizon glow, grid on the ground following the slope, neon edges on the models, glowing target outline and zones.
3. The four weathers (clear, haze, neon rain, glowing snow) look different.
4. Quality Low, Medium, High: bloom off on Low, on for the others; the frame rate stays usable.
5. Wind 0, 5 and 15 m/s: grass, leaves and flags still sway in the right direction.
6. Grass looks even over the range, with no clumps or bare stripes.
7. Scene tour: the highlighted parts glow pale yellow; the Optic camera shows the reticle with no bracket.
8. Leave the page and come back: the scene restarts, and no CPU or fan load remains after leaving.
