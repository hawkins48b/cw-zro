# Dope card layout: values past the scales, long header text, whole metre zeros, overlapping labels

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Branch:** task/136-139-dope-card-layout
- **Issues:** [#136](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/136), [#137](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/137), [#138](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/138), [#139](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/139)
- **Status:** Not in production yet

## Summary

Four dope card issues found in #108, in one branch. All four were already in production.

| Issue | Change |
|-------|--------|
| #136 | The drop line stops at the edge of the printed graph. A point past the edge is drawn as an arrow on the edge, with its true value. A points-of-aim mark past its target box is drawn the same way on the box edge |
| #137 | Header text that does not fit its field is shrunk, down to 7 pt, then cut with an ellipsis (`…`). Short text prints at 11 pt as before |
| #138 | The metric card prints near and far zero as whole metres, like the imperial card prints whole yards |
| #139 | Drop labels stay inside the graph, so the last one no longer covers the right axis numbers. Points-of-aim labels stay inside their box and do not cover each other or another mark |

**Release notes:**

- Dope cards for slow or subsonic loads stay readable: values past the printed scales are shown at the edge with an arrow.
- Long rifle, ammo and optic names are shortened to fit the card.
- The metric dope card prints near and far zero in whole metres (100 instead of 100.1).

## Implementation

The drawing code of both cards moved to [`dopeCardLayout.js`](../src/lib/utils/dopeCardLayout.js). [`dopeCardImperial.js`](../src/lib/utils/dopeCardImperial.js) and [`dopeCardMetric.js`](../src/lib/utils/dopeCardMetric.js) keep their trajectory code (`computeTrajectory`, unchanged) and pass the values in card units.

| Part | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| `CARD_LAYOUTS` | Printed scales of each template, in inches from the top left: graph (x of 0 and 500, y of drop 0, inches per unit, top and bottom values) and the four target boxes (centre, edges, inches per MOA or mrad). The box edges and centres were read from the template drawings with pdf.js; the graph values are the ones the cards used before |
| `HEADER_FIELDS`, `fitText`, `drawHeaderText` | Each header value has a start and a right limit (the box edge or the printed unit). `fitText` measures the text with the embedded Helvetica Bold, shrinks it to fit, rounded down to 0.1 pt, and below 7 pt cuts characters and adds `…` |
| `drawDropGraph` | Clips each line segment to the band between the top and bottom of the scale (`clipSegmentY`). A point inside gets its dot and label, below the point as before, above it when that would leave the graph, and moved left when it would pass the right edge. A point past an edge gets an arrow on that edge. Its label sits inside the graph next to the edge, in two alternating rows so that neighbours 25 units apart do not overlap |
| `drawPointsOfAim` | Places the elevation and the two windage marks, clamped to the box, with an arrow when clamped. Each label tries up to four places (light windage: left, below, above, right; strong windage: right, below, above, left; elevation: right, left, above, below). It takes the first inside the box that touches no placed label and no other mark, then the first inside the box that touches no label, then the first one clamped into the box |

The values printed are the same as before. Only where they are drawn changed, and the metric zeros (#138).

## Security review

Round 1: PASS, with two Low findings in this change, both fixed:

| Finding | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| The ellipsis loop measured the whole text once per removed character: a pasted name of 30000 characters froze the page for about 21 s | `fitText` starts the cut from at most 200 characters; no field holds 100 at 7 pt |
| Measuring a text with a line break or tab throws, where `drawText` alone printed it: a damaged stored name failed the card | `drawHeaderText` prints control characters (U+0000 to U+001F, U+007F, U+2028, U+2029) as spaces |

Round 2: **PASS**. A 30000 character name now takes about 5 ms, and a million narrow characters about 0.1 s.

Pre-existing, reported and not fixed: any character outside WinAnsi (emoji, CJK, Cyrillic, Polish ł, U+202F, C1 controls such as U+0085) in a header field fails the card, as before; the profile text fields have no `maxlength`.

## Checked by eye

The cards were rendered to PNG (pdf.js in Chromium) for ten profiles on both templates, among them the subsonic load, a 500 yd zero whose path rises above +20 in, a 300 BLK 200 m metric zero, and the long names of #108. The subsonic card shows the line stopping at -80 in with arrows and values for 275 to 500 yd, and the points-of-aim elevations above 20 MOA pinned to the top of their boxes.

## Limitations

- The labels of points past an edge sit in two rows near that edge. They can cover the grid there, and on a steep curve the label of the last point inside can sit near them. They do not leave the graph.
- When no free place is found for a points-of-aim label, it is clamped into the box and may touch a mark. The test profiles never reach that case.
- The 7 pt minimum and the place order are my choices, not the owner's.
- A name with characters Helvetica cannot encode (for example emoji) still makes the card fail with "could not be created", as before this change ([#123](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/123)).
- Not printed on paper: the "printed" criterion of #108 is still the owner's.
