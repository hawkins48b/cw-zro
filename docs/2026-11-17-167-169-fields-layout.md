## #168 Hold − / + to repeat

`StepButton` uses [`holdRepeat.js`](../src/lib/utils/holdRepeat.js):

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Press | One step at once |
| Held 400 ms | Repeats every 100 ms, one step each |
| After 10 repeats (about 1.4 s held) | 10 steps each repeat |
| Release, pointer leaves the button, touch cancel (scroll) | Stops |
| Enter / Space (click with `detail` 0) | One step |
| Pointer click after a press | Ignored (the press already stepped) |
| Right / middle button | Nothing |

`onclick` now gets the number of steps. `StepperField` and the cant buttons apply `stepValue` that many times, so the limits of `fieldStep.js` hold at each step. The button has `touch-action: manipulation`, no text selection, no iOS callout, and its `contextmenu` is prevented. The timings are my defaults, stated on the issue, not confirmed by the owner.

## #169 One page frame

The root layout's content area now gives every page the same frame (values from the design boards: 1180 px centred, phone 16 px):

| Width | Side padding | Top / bottom |
|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Under 640 px | 16 px | 0 / 16 px |
| 640 to 1023 px | 24 px (in line with the header) | 0 / 24 px |
| 1024 px and over | `clamp(16px, 3vw, 40px)` | 32 / 48 px |

Inside it the content is centred within 1180 px on every device. All page roots drop their own `max-w-*`, `mx-auto` and `px-*` and use `flex flex-col gap-3 sm:gap-4 lg:gap-5`: Scope View, Calculators, Ballistic, Dope Cards, MPBR, Validation, Compare, Profiles, Ammo selector, profile editor (`ProfileForm`) and its not-found state, Settings, About, Licences, Privacy. Compare › Add and the precision guide stay a 672 px column, now centred. The Settings tablet layout drops its own `md:px-4`. Welcome and setup are bare routes and were not changed.

Before, desktop pages were left-aligned except Scope View, Ballistic, MPBR and Validation; widths were 672, 1180, 1200 or 1240 px; phone gutters were 8 or 16 px.
