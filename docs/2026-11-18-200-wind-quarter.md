# 2026-11-18: Precision card: bigger wind quarter, inner ring on its ring when it fits

Issue: [#200](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/200). Branch `task/200-wind-quarter`. Owner request: the ⑥ wind quarter of the range pages left room under it, and the inner-ring values sat off their ring even when short.

## What changed

| Item | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Outer ring radius | 200 px (about 53 mm) | 236 px (about 62 mm); rings at 59, 118, 177, 236 px |
| Dial centre on the page | 91.5, 480 px | 60, 510 px (px of the 397 px wide design page, y down) |
| "90°" label | under III | after III, as "II 60°" and "I 30°" |
| Inner ring values on 15°, 45°, 75° | always 22 px outside the ring | on the ring when every inner value fits there; else 26 px outside |

- `src/lib/utils/precisionCardLayout.js`: the dial is given from its centre (`origin` is the centre, `centre` is 0, 0) and its labels are placed from the outer radius `DIAL_R`. New helpers `dialValueBox`, `dialRingLabelBox` and `dialInnerFits`; `dialValuePoint(ring, ray, staggered)` takes the stagger choice.
- `src/lib/utils/precisionCardPdf.js`: on each range page, the fill measures the inner ring's values (Helvetica Bold widths) and staggers the ring only if one value box (with its halo) would overlap another or the ring speed on the XII axis. One-digit values always fit; two-digit values on 15° to 60° do not (30° and 45° are too close at 59 px), so a mix fits or not by page.
- `static/pdf/precision-{en,fr,de}.pdf` regenerated with `npm run templates:precision`.
- `messages/*.json` `dope_guide_wind_rays`: the guide says the inner numbers sit outside the ring only when they are too long to fit side by side.

No calculated value changed: only positions.
