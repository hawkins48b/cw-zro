# Precision card wind dial: second ring lined up (#174)

Owner request, [#174](https://github.com/hawkins48b/cw-zro/issues/174). Follows #159.

## Change

| What | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| `RANGE.dial.stagger` (`src/lib/utils/precisionCardLayout.js`) | `[22, 12, 0, 0]` | `[22, 0, 0, 0]` |
| `RANGE.dial.halo.padding` | 2 px | 1 px |
| Guide text `dope_guide_wind_rays` (en, fr, de) | "the two inner rings" | "the inner ring" |

- On the range pages, values on the second ring now sit on the ring on every ray, as they already did on the outer two rings. The inner ring keeps its stagger: there, neighbouring rays are only about 12 px apart.
- With the 2 px halo, the existing overlap test failed on one worst case: a 3000 yd card in MOA, angle mode, with "10.0" and "19.4" on the 15° and 30° rays of ring 2. The digits were 2.7 px apart, but the white halos overlapped by about 1 px. With a 1 px halo, every case of the #159 overlap test passes: 3000 m and 3000 yd, clicks and angle mode, metric, imperial and mixed units. The halo is smaller on every ring.
- The templates are unchanged: values are drawn when the card is filled.
- No calculated value changed.

## Tests

- New spec in `src/test/precisionCardPdf.test.js`: from the second ring on, every value is exactly on its ring, and only the inner ring is staggered on 15°, 45° and 75°.
- `npm run test:run`: 64 files, 4905 tests passed. `npm run build` passes.

## Not checked

- Not checked on paper or in a PDF viewer. The owner should print a card and look at the second ring, mainly the 15° and 30° values at long range.
- The security review was skipped: only a layout constant and guide text changed, and no input is handled.
